# Benchmark

15 SNAP graphs as simple undirected graphs (no self-loops, no parallel edges). Accuracy is the density found divided by the optimal density computed by Goldberg's method, truncated to two decimals: 100.00% means that the optimum was found. Generated by `densest report` from `benchmark.csv`.

## Overview

| algorithm                            | worst accuracy   | mean accuracy   | optimum found   | vs greedy (better/equal/worse)   | total time (s)   | geometric mean time (s)   |
|:-------------------------------------|:-----------------|:----------------|:----------------|:---------------------------------|:-----------------|:--------------------------|
| Goldberg (exact)                     | 100.00%          | 100.00%         | 15/15           | 10/5/0                           | 39.8             | 0.111                     |
| Charikar LP (exact)                  | 100.00%          | 100.00%         | 13/15           | 8/5/0                            | ≥ 1574.2         | ≥ 2.412                   |
| Greedy peeling                       | 92.23%           | 99.06%          | 5/15            | 0/15/0                           | 35.6             | 0.097                     |
| Greedy++                             | 99.73%           | 99.96%          | 11/15           | 10/5/0                           | 437.9            | 0.871                     |
| Spectral-Charikar, thesis thresholds | 90.52%           | 97.50%          | 3/15            | 3/2/10                           | 4.5              | 0.045                     |
| Spectral-Charikar                    | 90.69%           | 97.59%          | 3/15            | 3/2/10                           | 1.3              | 0.015                     |
| Spectral-Charikar + refinement       | 98.75%           | 99.87%          | 9/15            | 8/5/2                            | 6.1              | 0.020                     |

Charikar LP (exact) did not finish within 600 s on musae_squirrel, twitch_gamers: its accuracy is over the other graphs, and its times count the limit.
